Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: alimirza00@gmail.com

App Description

Deen Time is a mobile application that provides:

  • Salah times
  • Duas
  • Qibla direction
  • Tasbeeh counter
  • Alarm of Salah
  • Activity tracking

Permissions

To provide the best user experience, Deen Time may request the following permissions:

  • INTERNET - to access online content and services.
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION & ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION - to determine your location for accurate Salah times and Qibla direction.
  • WAKE_LOCK - to ensure alarms and notifications function properly.
  • SET_ALARM - to schedule Salah alarms.
  • VIBRATE - to alert you with vibration for alarms and notifications.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE & CHANGE_WIFI_STATE - to detect network connectivity and improve performance.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM - to schedule precise alarms for Salah times.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS - to send you reminders and alerts.
